What the columns mean
Scope
Each token once: its first call (repeat calls and update posts are left out). Returns are measured from the price 60 seconds after the post (late entry), in USD, before any buy or sell tax. Calls tracked in another asset (no USD price) and calls not tracked are counted under Calls but have no numbers.
Window
Every number is for the window chosen above (1h, 1d, 3d, 7d or 30d after the call) and counts only the calls that have a recorded return for it.
Calls
First calls in the group (after the "Quiet after the call" choice).
With data
Of those, the calls with a return for the window: the n of every number in the row. Hover over it to see why the others have none: not due yet (the window has not passed), due but no data (the price source had no trades for it), waiting for the tracker, not tracked, or not priced in USD.
Mean, Median
Average and middle over the window: of the return, of the peak (the highest price within the window, from the entry) and of the drop (the lowest). A few very large winners pull the mean far up; the median is the typical call.
Win rate
Share of the calls with data whose return is above 0%. Under each rate: how many of how many calls.
≥ +100% peak
Share whose highest price within the window was at least double the entry (peak ≥ +100%), counted over the calls with a peak. A rugged call's peak before the rug counts.
Collapse
Share whose return over the window is −50% or worse.
Rugged
Calls with data that the tracker has flagged rugged (pool drained) as of now, not as of the window's end. The returns after a rug count as −100%.
Perceptor verdict at the call
The verdict of the call's own Perceptor scan, or of the latest earlier scan of the token, made when it was called. Later scans (a repeat call's, or a re-scan long after the call) are never used here, unlike the list's Perceptor column, which shows the token's latest scan. Calls imported from history were not scanned.
Pool family
The kind of pool the tracker priced the call from: Uniswap v2, v3, v4 or the Pons launch curve. "GeckoTerminal" = priced from GeckoTerminal candles (no on-chain pool); "Not tracked" = no entry price.
DEX in the post
The DEX name the call post gives, as written: the most frequent ones, the rest under "Other". Names change over time (for example "Pons" until mid-August, then "Pons V2").
Quiet after the call
A call with fewer than 50 trades in the 24 hours after it (counted from the stored 5-minute candles; for Uniswap v2 pools liquidity changes count too). Rugged calls are never counted as quiet. The count is known only for calls priced from an on-chain pool whose first day the tracker has stored; for the others (GeckoTerminal, untracked, not yet a day old) it is unknown, and "Exclude" keeps them while "Only quiet" leaves them out.
Week, Month
By the time of the call in UTC. Weeks run Monday to Sunday ("Week of Mon 3 Aug 2026").
grey
A group with fewer than 20 calls with data: too few for its numbers to mean much.
Metric, Statistic
What the hour × weekday chart and the "By factor" charts show: the return at the end of the window, the peak (highest price within it) or the drawdown (lowest price within it), each from the entry; and whether a cell or a line is the mean or the median. The trend, outcome mix and "$100 on every call" charts always use the return.
Pool family, Verdict at the call
Show only the calls of one pool family or of one Perceptor verdict at the call. They apply to every table and chart, except "$100 on every call": its "All calls" and "No red flags at the call" lines ignore both (they follow only the quiet choice), and it adds a line for the chosen pool family and verdict.
Trend
Per week (or month): bars = first calls; lines = mean and median return of the calls with data, and the collapse rate (share with a return of −50% or worse; right axis). Hover over a week for its numbers. Weeks with fewer than 20 calls with data have hollow points.
Outcome mix
Shares of the calls with data per week (or month), by the return at the window: rugged (flagged rugged by the tracker, whatever the return), −50% or worse, above −50% up to 0%, above 0% to below +100%, +100% or more.
$100 on every call
The running profit or loss if $100 had gone into every call with data, in the order of the calls, and come out at the end of the window: each call adds its return in dollars, capped at +1,000% (+$1,000), so one huge winner does not hide the rest; before tax and fees. Lines: all calls and the calls with no red flags at the time of the call (both follow the quiet choice only, not the pool family or verdict choice), and, when a pool family or verdict is chosen, the calls of that choice (when only "No red flags" is chosen, that line is already drawn).
Hour × weekday
When the call was posted (UTC). Each cell: the mean or median of the metric over the calls with data posted in that hour of that weekday; hover for the number of calls. Cells with fewer than 20 calls with data are grey.
By factor
Values known at the time of the call: the Perceptor verdict; the market cap at the call, worked out as the price at the post × the token's total supply read from the chain (at the call's block when the node still has that state, else the current supply), so fully diluted (Pons launch-curve tokens include the supply still unsold on the curve), not the post's "called at" figure; the holders, elite holders and good holders the post counts; the elite and good live buys it lists (count and dollars); and the hour of trading before the call, measured on the chain (buy and sell volume in USD, swaps, price change). Number factors are split into groups of about the same number of calls (4, 5 or 10), over the calls with data for the window that have a value; equal values stay in one group, so groups can differ in size; when at least 10% of the values are 0, 0 is a group of its own and the values below and above 0 are split separately (so no group spans 0), sharing the other groups by their number of calls, at least one group for each side that has values (so there are still 4, 5 or 10 groups in total when the values differ enough). Calls without a value are left out. Pre-call volumes are shown only for calls whose hour was measured in USD (some were measured in the pool's quote asset).
Distribution bands
A value on a boundary goes to the band farther from 0: a band below 0 includes its upper end (−50 is in "−75 to −50", 0 itself in "−25 to 0"), a band above 0 its lower end (+25 is in "+25 to +50"), and "0 to +25" starts just above 0. So the two lowest bands are the collapses, the bands above 0 the wins, and the two highest the calls at +100% or more. Each bar is a share of its group; all groups use the same axes.
Scales
Returns, peaks and drops are drawn on a symmetric log scale (−100%, −50%, 0, +100%, +1,000% …), so a few huge winners do not flatten the rest. Dollar amounts and counts are drawn on a log scale that keeps 0.
Typical path
The mean or median of the metric at 1h, 1d, 3d, 7d and 30d after the call, per group. "All with data at each point" uses every call with data at that point (n changes from point to point: the table under the chart gives it); "Same calls at every point" uses only the calls with data at all five.
Peak vs final
Each call with data: its peak within the window against its return at the end of it. A call "gave back more than half" when its peak was above 0% and its return is less than half of the peak.
Timing
When things happened after the call, measured from the stored price candles: 5-minute steps on the first day, hourly after, so a time is known to about 5 minutes (1 hour after the first day). Times are counted from the post. Only first calls, priced in USD, with a return for the window and their timing computed ("n timed"); calls whose timing is not computed yet (tracked before it existed, until the owner's backfill runs) are left out and counted. These are results, never used as inputs of the model.
Time to peak
When the highest price within the window (from the late entry) was first reached; 0 when the entry itself was the peak.
Reached 2×
Reached 2× = at or above 2× for at least 1 hour in total within the window (the time held at 2×, below), in every window: a call still at 2× when the window ended needs it too. A call that touched twice the entry price (a candle's high) without reaching 2× by this rule, including a touch and drop (0 held), counts as not reaching 2×, like a call that never touched it: it stays in every total (so the share that reached 2× is not inflated) but is left out of the time to first 2×, the time to fall below 2× and the "still at 2× after t" curve, while its real time held (under 1 hour) still counts in the time held at 2× figures. Time to first 2×: when the price of a call that reached 2× first touched it, over those calls. The 1h window shows "n/a — needs 1 hour held" for the reached-2× figures: the time held is counted from the end of the first 2× candle, which closes after the post, so no call there can hold 2× for an hour.
Time held at 2×
How long the price stayed at or above 2× the entry, measured on candle closes (the last close carried across quiet stretches). Every call that touched 2× counts the time it really held, also one that held it for less than 1 hour and so did not reach 2× by the rule above (owner decision, 13 October 2026); a call that never touched 2× counts as 0 held. In the histogram, blue bars are the calls that reached 2× (1 hour or more), amber bars the calls that touched 2× but held it for less than 1 hour, and the grey 0 bar the calls with 0 held (never touched 2×, or touched it without a candle closing at or above it).
Time to fall below 2×
When the price first closed under 2× the entry after reaching it (or the rug, when the pool was drained while it was at 2×), counted from the post. Calls still at 2× when the window ended have no fall time: they are "censored".
Still at 2× after t
Of the calls that reached 2× (held at least 1 hour in total), the share still at or above 2× t after first reaching it (Kaplan–Meier). t is the time held at 2× on closes, but at most the time from the first 2× (the start of its candle) to the first close under 2× or the rug, so a call that fell back and rose again counts only its first stretch and can fall back before 1 hour. A call still at 2× when the window ended is censored: counted until then, not as fallen back. "Half fell back by" is the curve's median: the t where it first drops to 50% or below.
Rug times
"Event": the pool was seen drained by a scan, at the exact time of the rug block; only these are in the time-to-rug histogram and the "share rugged by t" curve, and only when the rug came within the window. "At the call": drained at or before the post (no time after the call). "Detected": found only by the check at the end of tracking; its time is that check's, so it is only an upper bound. Both are counted beside the histogram, never in it.
Share rugged by t
Of the timed calls, the share whose pool was drained (event rug times only) within t of the post. Every timed call was followed for the whole window, so this is a plain running share.
Rounding
Every return, peak and drop is rounded to 0.1% before the thresholds above are applied: a return of +0.04% counts as 0.0% (not a win), −49.96% as −50.0% (a collapse), a peak of +99.96% as +100.0%.
–
No call in the group has a value for this number.

Top 10% = scored above 90% of the calls the model was tested on when it was trained (so the share can drift from 10%). Runner rates and returns are before tax (training labels are after tax).
