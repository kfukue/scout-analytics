What the statuses mean
pending
Not processed yet.
tracking
Entry price found. The horizons that have passed are recorded; the tracker is waiting for the later ones.
done
All five horizons (1h, 1d, 3d, 7d, 30d) are recorded. A call needs to be 30 days old to get here.
no pool
No trading pool found for the token yet. Retried later.
error
Something failed, for example a node error. Retried later.
gave up
More than 30 days old and still no pool or no trades.
rugged
Shown after the status when the pool was drained or the price fell below 5% of entry.
Perceptor
The latest on-chain check of the token by the Perceptor bot; the words link to its report. no red flags = the check found none. caution = it found something to look at before buying. red flags = it found serious warning signs. Only calls picked up live are scanned: calls imported from history were never scanned, so they show a dash (–).
▸ and sA
▸ before the date (or a click on the row) opens the token's reports under the row: the latest Perceptor report (verdict, summary and link) and the latest sAlpha report (its text and link). sA next to the Perceptor verdict = the token has an sAlpha report. About half of sAlpha's replies are empty; an empty reply counts as no report, so the latest reply with text is shown, or "No sAlpha report".
Latest %
The return at the most recent price, measured from the same entry (60 seconds after the post), followed by how old the call was when that price was read, for example 45m, 30h or 60d. It is refreshed about every 15 minutes for calls under 30 days old and once a day for older ones. The window selector does not affect it. quiet = no trade in the 7 days before the price was read, so it is the price of an older trade. Hover over the number for the exact times.
1h, 1d, 3d, 7d, 30d
The return over that window, in USD, measured from the price 60 seconds after the post (not the price in the post). A dash (–) until the window has passed and been recorded. Click a column to sort by it.
Peak %, Worst drop %
The highest and the lowest price within the window chosen with the "Peak / worst drop over" selector (1h to 30d), from the same entry. Only these two columns follow the selector.
Call MC
The market cap given in the call post: its "called at" figure, or its Mcap line when there is none or it is not a usable number (zero or below). Hover over the number for the exact amount.
Latest MC
An estimate, not a reading: the market cap in the post (its Mcap line, or the "called at" figure when there is none or it is not usable) × the latest price ÷ the price at the post. It assumes the token supply has not changed since the call. A dash (–) when the post gave no market cap or there is no latest price yet.
no USD price
The token trades against an asset with no dollar price, so no performance is shown.
–
No value yet: the window has not arrived, or the call is not tracked yet.
