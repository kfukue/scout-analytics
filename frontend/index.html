What the statuses mean
pending
Not processed yet.
tracking
Entry price found. The horizons that have passed are recorded; the tracker is waiting for the later ones.
done
All five horizons (1h, 1d, 3d, 7d, 30d) are recorded. A call needs to be 30 days old to get here.
no pool
No trading pool found for the token yet. Retried later.
error
Something failed, for example a node error. Retried later.
gave up
More than 30 days old and still no pool or no trades.
rugged
Shown after the status when the pool was drained or the price fell below 5% of entry.
Perceptor
The latest on-chain check of the token by the Perceptor bot; the words link to its report. no red flags = the check found none. caution = it found something to look at before buying. red flags = it found serious warning signs. Only calls picked up live are scanned: calls imported from history were never scanned, so they show a dash (–).
no USD price
The token trades against an asset with no dollar price, so no performance is shown.
–
No value yet: the horizon has not arrived, or the call is not tracked yet.
